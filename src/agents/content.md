# Content Plan

## Product Positioning

> Create, sell, and fulfill custom products without managing inventory, production, or shipping.

The initial focus is print-on-demand tumblers, with more products and suppliers added later.

## Primary Users

- Users who run one or more shops on TikTok Shop, Shopee, Shopify, or similar (often several shops on the same marketplace).
- Admins who review designs, orders, and fulfillment issues.
- Designers who prepare or maintain reusable product artwork.

## Public Website

### Home

- Clear value proposition
- Product/lifestyle hero image
- “How it works” overview
- Benefits: no inventory, custom designs, supplier fulfillment
- Featured products
- Shop success or workflow section
- Primary CTA: **Start selling**

### How It Works

1. Choose a product.
2. Customize the design.
3. Publish it to your shop.
4. Receive orders.
5. We produce and fulfill them.

### Product Catalog

- Product categories
- Product specifications
- Base cost and estimated fulfillment cost
- Available countries and shipping information
- Product mockups

### Design Studio

The user-facing design tool should support:

- Uploading images
- Adding, editing, and styling text
- Moving, resizing, rotating, and cropping elements
- Templates and reusable design presets
- Product mockup preview
- Print-area boundaries and safe zones
- Resolution and file-quality validation
- Saving designs and assigning them to SKUs
- Automatic PNG and TIFF generation

The MVP should focus on product customization rather than becoming a full Canva replacement.

### Pricing

- Product and fulfillment cost
- Estimated shipping cost
- Suggested selling price
- Estimated shop margin
- Transparent explanation of additional fees

### Integrations

Start with a clear “coming soon” or onboarding flow for TikTok Shop and Shopee. Add Shopify and other channels later.

### FAQ and Support

Cover design requirements, production time, shipping, returns, refunds, copyright responsibility, supported countries, and payments.

## User Workspace

- Dashboard overview
- Design library
- Design Studio
- Product and SKU management
- Shops (many per user, several per marketplace allowed)
- Order management
- Fulfillment status
- Revenue and margin summary
- Billing, wallet, or prepaid balance

## Admin Platform

- New-order review queue
- Design and SKU verification
- Failed conversion alerts
- MQC/warehouse handoff
- Order exceptions and returns
- User & shop management
- Reconciliation reports

## Later Features

- Automated TikTok Shop and Shopee synchronization
- Multi-warehouse routing
- Shipment tracking
- Supplier marketplace
- Analytics and sales reports
- Team roles and approvals
- AI-assisted mockup and design suggestions
