FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still unsure? Talk to our team — we reply within 30 minutes.

Is there a minimum order?

No. You can order a single piece. Every order, big or small, gets the same print and QC process.

Which platforms do you integrate with?

Shopify, Etsy, TikTok Shop, Amazon, WooCommerce and eBay. You can also import orders by CSV, use our API or place manual orders.

How fast do orders ship?

Most orders are printed within 24 hours and delivered in 3–5 business days, so up to about 6 days from checkout to doorstep.

Can my buyers see printexo on the package?

No. We ship blind by default. You can add custom neck labels, packing inserts and branded mailers.

What print methods do you use?

DTG and DTF for apparel, embroidery for caps and patches, sublimation for mugs and all-over prints, and vinyl for stickers.

What if a print is wrong or damaged?

Send us a photo. We reprint and reship at no cost to you — usually the same day.
