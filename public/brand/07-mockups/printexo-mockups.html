The brief

Print-on-demand for online shops: upload a design, list it on TikTok Shop, Shopee or Shopify, and printexo prints and ships every order. exo means connecting: printexo is the joint between the shop, the print floor and the customer.

Success: an abstract symbol for connecting, built from the brand's one 30° blade angle, that works at 16 px, in one colour and next to the existing custom wordmark.

Final design

printexo v3 · Dovetail

Two halves of a square locked by a tail that flares at the 30° blade angle: a joint that holds without glue.

  • Says connecting without drawing an object: exo is the joint
  • Built from the same 30° blade as the wordmark, so symbol and lettering read as one system
  • A solid square, so it is the strongest possible app icon and favicon; the Lime tail marks the connection
